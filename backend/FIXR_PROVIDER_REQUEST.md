# Draft for FIXR partnership enquiry

**To:** FIXR business or support contact, once confirmed

**Subject:** Permission and supported flow for Aftermarket ticket transfers

Hello FIXR team,

Aftermarket is exploring a consent-based resale workflow, starting with a small Oxford pilot and potentially expanding to South Africa. We want to use FIXR's official ticket transfer feature only where the organiser has enabled it, and to avoid duplicate or invalid tickets.

Could you tell us:

1. Whether FIXR permits a third-party marketplace to facilitate paid resale using customer transfer links, and what written agreement is required.
2. Whether a business account, partner API, webhook, or other approved way exists to confirm that the buyer claimed a transfer and that the original ticket was invalidated.
3. Whether a ticket can be transferred more than once, and what rules or event-specific restrictions apply.
4. Whether FIXR supports a service inbox or account acting as an intermediary, or requires direct seller-to-buyer transfer.
5. How refunds, cancelled events, disputes and buyer identity checks should be handled in this flow.

We will keep all ticket movement manual and will not automate FIXR account access while these points are unresolved.

Regards,
Aftermarket team
