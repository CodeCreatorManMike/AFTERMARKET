# Oxford FIXR Halloween pilot — 24 October to 1 November 2026

The app shows a manually reviewed selection of 14 public FIXR event pages from 24, 30 and 31 October, recorded in [`app/src/data/oxfordFixrEvents.ts`](../app/src/data/oxfordFixrEvents.ts). These are direct event links with date-only labels, not a live feed. No Oxford FIXR event for 25 October or 1 November was verified from the public pages reviewed; this is **not** an exhaustive claim about all events. The app hides past events and asks buyers to check the official page for time, availability, venue, age rules and transfer eligibility. Sold-out notes reflect the page when reviewed on 24 October and may change.

Public discovery sources: [Bridge Oxford organiser](https://fixr.co/organiser/BridgeOxford?lang=en-US), [Oxford events organiser](https://fixr.co/organiser/theoxevents?lang=en-US), [Plush Oxford venue](https://fixr.co/venue/plush-oxford-oxford-6481?lang=en-US), and [FIXR venue listing](https://fixr.co/venue/1289). Direct URLs are stored alongside each event. The original Halloween hero illustration is [`app/assets/event-images/oxford-halloween-pilot.png`](../app/assets/event-images/oxford-halloween-pilot.png).

This is curated public-page research rather than an automated scraper. FIXR's [customer terms](https://fixr.co/legal/customer-terms-and-conditions) restrict automated extraction and commercial account use. A production catalogue requires a permitted data source, freshness checks, and organiser approval. The app's local screenshot preview is not an upload to a server; no public listing, checkout, inbox polling or ticket transfer is live. The [backend pilot](../backend/README.md) records the proposed gates and provider integration seams.
